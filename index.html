The atelier

Every room begins
with one object.

A carved headboard. A table in natural ebony. A chandelier cast in bronze. Nabel Elmasry builds each interior around one piece of craft, and lets the rest of the room make space for it.

The studio furnishes residences in Cairo, on the Red Sea coast and across the Gulf, working directly with two Spanish workshops: Franco Furniture and Soher.
